# Accounting Practice CRM in Australia: Connect the Apps Your Firm Already Uses

A CRM should give an accounting or finance practice one reliable view of the relationship. Who the client is, what they need, who owns the next action and where the opportunity or engagement sits.

It should not become another database that staff update because management asked them to. The commercial value comes from connecting the client journey around the systems the team already relies on.

## What should an accounting practice CRM manage?

A useful practice CRM gives the firm a clear view of prospects and clients from first enquiry through onboarding and ongoing service. It should help staff see who owns the relationship, what happened last, what is waiting, and what needs to happen next.

That can include enquiry capture, qualification, appointment booking, client communication, document requests, workflow stages, reminders, pipeline visibility and internal notifications.

## Work around the accounting stack

Australian practices commonly operate across several systems. Xero, MYOB and QuickBooks may sit at the centre of client accounting, while calendars. Cloud storage, email, proposal tools and specialist apps handle other parts of the client journey.

The goal of a CRM layer is not to recreate every ledger function. It is to connect the operational work around those systems so the practice has fewer gaps between tools.

AccrualOS is designed around this principle. Sharktech describes it as one connected platform for enquiries, client administration and practice workflows.

## Integration should follow the workflow

Do not integrate software simply because an API exists. Start with a business outcome. If a new client books a meeting, what information should move into the CRM? If onboarding is approved, which task should be created? If a document arrives, who needs to know?

A good integration removes duplicate entry and creates a reliable next action.

## Xero, MYOB and QuickBooks belong in the wider ecosystem

Xero, MYOB and QuickBooks already provide accounting and bookkeeping capabilities. AccrualOS should be positioned as the practice operating layer around the accounting work, subject to the specific integrations and authorisations configured for each firm.

That distinction is important. Accountants should not be told to abandon the software they trust simply to gain better client workflow automation.

## Smart automation for accountants

Smart automation connects events to useful actions. A new enquiry can create a CRM record. A qualified prospect can receive a booking option. A completed consultation can start onboarding. Missing information can trigger an approved reminder. An exception can be handed to a person.

The automation supports the team. It does not replace professional judgement.

## CRM value comes from data quality and decision visibility

For a CFO, a CRM is useful when it improves the quality of pipeline and client information. That means clear ownership, consistent stages, reliable lead source, complete contact data and a defined next action.

The most important design decision is the source of truth. Xero, MYOB or QuickBooks may remain the accounting system. A proposal tool may remain the source for engagement documents. The CRM should coordinate the client relationship without pretending to own every piece of data.

Measure whether the CRM improves:

- enquiry response and follow-up
- pipeline conversion
- onboarding cycle time
- client handovers
- cross-service opportunities
- overdue actions
- management visibility

For finance brokers and CFO advisory firms, the same discipline applies. The workflow may involve lenders, applications, funding milestones or recurring advisory work rather than tax engagements. But the CRM should still make ownership and next actions obvious.

Integration should be approved because it removes duplicate entry or reduces risk, not simply because a connector exists. That is the difference between a useful operating system and a collection of integrations.

## CRM discipline improves forecast quality

A pipeline is only useful when the stages mean something. If staff move opportunities inconsistently or leave records without a next action, the forecast will look more precise than it really is.

For accounting and finance practices, define the entry and exit criteria for each stage. A qualified enquiry should mean the same thing across the team. An onboarding stage should not be marked complete while critical information is missing. That discipline improves management reporting and helps a CFO distinguish real pipeline from activity that is unlikely to convert.
